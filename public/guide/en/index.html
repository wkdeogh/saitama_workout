Record your workouts. Watch your character change.

BBAKBAK Training is a web workout log. Record what you actually did today, then see your accumulated exercise reflected in your character. You can start without signing in.

What the app does

Log push-ups, squats, other exercise repetitions and running distance by date. Review past workouts on the calendar and earn EXP that changes your character’s level and appearance. Goal completion and exercise volume are calculated separately, so a workout still counts even when you do not reach every goal.

The Outdoor Gym is a shared virtual space for showing your character to other users. It serves a different purpose from Training, where you record actual exercise.

Your first workout record

  1. Choose your goals. Change them in Settings or the user guide.
  2. Enter your actual workout. Running is measured in kilometres, in increments of 0.1km.
  3. Save the workout. If you save again that day, enter the complete daily total.
  4. Check your character and calendar. Goal completion and earned EXP are shown separately.

A partial workout still counts

Example: your first day

40 push-ups + 30 squats + 20 other exercise reps + a 1km run

40 + 30 + 20 + 20 = 110 EXP

A character with no previous records moves from LV. 1 to LV. 2 and keeps 10 EXP toward the next level. These exercises count even if the goals remain 100 reps and 10km.

Start locally, then continue with an account

Guest records are saved in the current browser. Signing in lets you carry those records into your account and synchronize them. To continue on another device, sign in to the same account and check the sync status.

The app organizes exercise you have completed. The initial goals of 100 reps and 10km are editable starting values, not a universal exercise prescription. Set goals appropriate to your situation.
