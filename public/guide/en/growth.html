Exercise, goal completion and streaks are separate.

Recorded exercise drives your level. Goal completion compares the record with your targets. Streak rewards are a separate change based on consecutive workout days.

Converting exercise into EXP

Each push-up, squat or other exercise rep earns 1 EXP. Running earns 2 EXP per 0.1km, or 20 EXP per kilometre. Your goal size and completion status do not change these rates.

Daily EXP example

30 push-ups + 20 squats + 10 other exercise reps + a 1.5km run

30 + 20 + 10 + 30 = 90 EXP

All recorded exercise counts, even when only some goals are complete.

Levels and appearance

Every 100 EXP raises your level by one, up to LV. 1000. If you have 70 EXP toward the next level and earn another 50, you gain one level and retain 20 EXP. At the maximum level there is no next-level EXP to accumulate.

The Evolution collection shows changes to body shape, muscles, hair, clothing and energy effects. Locked stages appear as silhouettes until you reach their required levels.

Workout streak rewards

A streak counts consecutive days with a recorded workout worth more than zero EXP. Reaching every goal is not required to maintain it.

Your aura upgrades every 10 days, with rewards changing through day 60. Fiery eyes unlock at 30 days and teal fiery eyes at 50 days. Missing a day breaks the streak, so these rewards are separate from appearance unlocked by character level.

Taking time away from recording

From the fifth day without a workout, you lose one level per day, with a minimum of LV. 1. If you record on Monday and do not record again, Tuesday is day one without a workout and Saturday is day five. Level loss starts on Saturday.

Recording exercise again updates the last workout date. Inactivity does not erase your accumulated exercise, so calculating your current level from lifetime EXP alone can give a different result from the displayed level.
