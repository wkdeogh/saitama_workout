Answers to common recording and account questions.

Start here when something looks different from what you expected. If these answers do not resolve the issue, contact us with details of your device and browser.

Why did I earn EXP without reaching my goals?

This is expected. EXP is calculated from recorded exercise, while goal completion separately compares that record with its saved targets. Lowering a goal can change completion status, but does not award extra EXP.

Why are my records missing on another device?

Guest records stay inside the browser. Sign in and finish syncing on the device where you originally recorded exercise, then sign in to the same account on the other device. Even when signed in, sync pending or an error message may mean the server has not received the latest changes.

Why did my level or aura change after a break?

Level loss starts on the fifth day without a workout, at one level per day. Auras and fiery eyes depend on your streak, so missing even one day can change them. Lifetime EXP, current level and consecutive workout days are different measures.

How do friend search and weekly rankings work?

Search the Friends tab using a nickname prefix or account tag. Tags distinguish users with the same nickname. Global rankings show up to 50 users; weekly rankings start Monday at midnight, Korea time.

How do Share and Copy link differ?

Workout proof saves today’s record and creates a character image. Share sends that image; Copy link copies the app address. Receiving an app link does not automatically grant access to your private workout records.

Can I add the app to my Home Screen?

On iPhone, choose Add to Home Screen from Safari’s Share menu. Enable Open as Web App if shown. iPhone push notifications require the Home Screen app. On Android Chrome, use Add to Home screen and choose Install or Create shortcut. Menu wording can vary by browser version.

Where can I report a problem?

Support: wkdeoghq@gmail.com

Include the screen and actions involved, the expected and actual results, and your device and browser. Hide email addresses and private conversations in screenshots. Do not send passwords or verification codes.
