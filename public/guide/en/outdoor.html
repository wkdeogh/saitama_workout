Meet other users as your character.

The Outdoor Gym is a shared virtual space for meeting other users and showing your character. Actions here are separate from real workout records.

Entering and leaving

Open the Outdoor Gym tab, review your character and select Enter. The 3D world fills the screen; Exit in the top-right returns you to the app. Guests can also meet other users connected to the same space.

Explore the grass field, trees, fenced workout area and surrounding track. Equipment such as the bench press and Smith machine forms part of the environment. Not every piece has a separate interaction or exercise animation.

Desktop and mobile controls

Basic world controls
FunctionControl
Desktop movementWASD or arrow keys
Mobile movementBottom-left joystick
Rotate the cameraDrag the world view
Walk or runToggle the running button

The default view is behind your character. Running is a movement-mode toggle: enable it, then use the movement controls to run. Toggle it again to return to walking.

Push-ups and squats

Press Push-ups or Squats to repeat the selected animation. Press the same button again or move your character to stop. During squats, the hands stay together at the chest and the knees open in the direction of the toes.

Virtual actions and real records

Even if your character performs several squats in the world, today’s squat count and EXP do not increase. Record exercise you actually completed in the Training tab and save it there.

Names and conversation

Nicknames appear above characters, and sent chat messages appear in speech bubbles. Other users’ names and bubbles become visible nearby and disappear at a distance.

Messages allow up to 80 characters and bubbles disappear after a short time. This is a public space; do not include personal information in chat. If other users are missing, check the shared-server connection status and try reconnecting later.
