Carry your local records into an account.

You can record workouts as a guest. Sign in to continue across devices, and distinguish records saved on your device from records synchronized to the server.

Where guest records live

Guest records stay in the browser on the device where they were saved. A different browser on the same phone does not automatically share those records. Clearing site data or ending a private-browsing session can remove records stored only on that device.

You can explore the basic features and record today’s workout before signing in. After signing in, check that your local records were carried over and complete synchronization.

When the account already has a record for the same date

Signing in carries local guest records into your account. When both sides contain the same date, the app takes the larger value for each exercise to avoid counting the same exercise twice.

Merging two records for one day

Account: 40 push-ups, 10 squats
Device: 25 push-ups, 30 squats

Result: 40 push-ups, 30 squats

Push-ups are not added into a total of 65. Records for different dates are carried over separately. Goals on an established account remain unchanged.

Checking synchronization

Use the person icon in the header to open My account. It contains your signed-in account, sync status, manual sync, sign-out and character-name editing.

When signed-in records change, the app attempts to synchronize automatically. Saved on this device / sync pending does not mean the server has received the changes. If a connection message appears, check your network and use Sync in My account to retry.

Check for Synced before moving to another device. Guest records and signed-in account records are stored separately.

Names and account status

Character names allow up to 20 characters. Edit yours in My account or with the pencil button on the Outdoor Gym entry screen. Signed-in character names, levels and exercise totals may appear in rankings.

Accounts with an applied super code display Super account status. It belongs to the account and remains after a name change or signing back in. It does not award extra levels or EXP. See the privacy policy for data handling and deletion enquiries.
