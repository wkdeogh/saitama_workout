Record the daily total. Choose goals that fit you.

Each field holds the total exercise completed today. Enter what you actually did and save it, whether or not you reached your goals.

Units and daily limits

Recording limits by exercise
ExerciseIncrementDaily maximum
Push-ups1 rep300 reps
Squats1 rep300 reps
Other exercises1 rep300 reps
Running0.1km60km

Other exercises are combined into one field. Its daily limit remains 300 reps even when you combine multiple activities. Running’s + and − controls change the value by 0.1km; quick-add buttons add 1km or 3km.

Updating a workout on the same day

40 morning reps, then 20 more

You saved 40 push-ups in the morning. After doing another 20, change the field to 60 and save. Entering 20 would replace today’s push-up total with 20.

Saving repeatedly does not create duplicate daily records. The app stores the final total for the day, rather than adding a new workout session each time.

Only today can be edited

You can enter, update and delete today’s exercise. Past records remain available on the calendar for viewing; missed dates cannot be filled in later. The recording date follows the date on the device running the app.

Check the selected date after midnight. If you are viewing an earlier record, use Today to return to the current day before entering exercise.

Changing your goals

Use Daily workout goals under the gear icon, or edit the values directly on the Workouts & goals page in the user guide. Moving to the next guide page also saves edited goals.

Push-up and squat goals range from 1 to 300 reps. Other exercises allow 0–300 reps, and running allows 0–60km. A goal of 0 makes other exercises or running optional for goal completion. Recording those exercises still earns EXP.

New goals apply to the current goals and today’s record. Targets stored in past records stay unchanged. Changing a goal does not increase your completed exercise or the EXP earned from it.
