1. Information and purposes

The Service processes information you enter, information supplied by sign-in providers, and information generated through use. It does not directly collect or store a separate password.

Feature / purpose Information and collection
Outdoor Gym On entry, real-time connections use an account or anonymous access identifier, session identifier, character name, level, streak, guest status, virtual position, direction, actions, connection time, and your latest speech bubble and its timestamp. Virtual movement is not real-world location information and does not affect workout records or EXP.
Workouts and character growth Your character name, exercise goals, daily push-ups, squats, other exercise reps and running distance; EXP, level, streak and total exercise calculated from those records. Guest records are stored in the browser and can be transferred and synced to your account after sign-in.
Authentication and account linking Firebase user identifier (UID), sign-in provider and linking information. Basic account information supplied by Google, such as email, name and profile image, may be used for Firebase authentication. Kakao sign-in receives a Kakao member number to link an account; Kakao email, nickname and profile photo are not requested.
Rankings and friends Account identifier, character name, account tag, level, EXP and exercise summaries, latest workout date, streak, friend list, request sender and recipient accounts, request status and timestamps, request counts and repeat-request restrictions.
Optional push notifications If you enable notifications and grant browser permission: device push token, installation identifier, recipient account, registration time and delivery information. Friend request notifications include the sender's character name and tag.
Service delivery and security Hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, browser and device information, request URLs and times, errors, authentication and operational logs. App settings and sync status are stored on the device.
Email inquiries Sender email address, inquiry content, account identifiers you provide and replies, used to handle inquiries, verify identity and process rights requests.

Google and Kakao information is received through their authentication processes. The Service currently has no feature that collects device GPS location, contacts, or camera or microphone contents.

2. Public information and sharing

Signed-in accounts with a registered character name participate in rankings. Visitors can view global rankings without signing in.

  • Other Outdoor Gym users, including guests, can see character names, appearances, actions and speech bubbles. Public character sync information, including access and session identifiers, level and streak, is also transmitted. Email and original workout records are not sent.
  • Rankings and character details display summaries such as character name, level, total and weekly EXP, total exercise by type, today's exercise and EXP, latest workout day and streak. Public responses may include account identifiers and tags.
  • Friend search and request handling show identification information such as character name and tag to other users. Email, sign-in tokens and complete original daily workout records are not made public in rankings.
  • Workout proof images are created on your device, without uploading them to the Service's server. If you choose to share or save, the image's name, date, exercise totals and level are passed to the app or recipient you select. That app's policies govern subsequent processing.

The operator does not sell personal information. Information is not provided to third parties except through the sharing and public features above, the processing arrangements below, separate user consent or a legal basis.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Outdoor Gym sessions: Only the latest position, actions and speech bubble are retained while connected; no conversation history list is stored. Bubbles appear for about five seconds and are deleted with the session when the server detects exit or disconnection. Anonymous guest authentication information may remain in the browser for reconnection.
  • Device records and settings: Retained until you delete the records or clear the site's device storage. Signing out alone does not erase all device records.
  • Account, workout, ranking and friend information: Retained while providing account services. A verified account deletion request removes the account's stored information and relationships. Individual workout records can be deleted in the app.
  • Friend requests and restrictions: Pending requests are deleted on acceptance, rejection or cancellation, and lists are updated. Daily request counts and repeat-request restrictions may remain during account use to prevent abuse. Expiry of a restriction does not itself automatically delete its stored information.
  • Push registrations: The relevant device registration is deleted from the server when notifications are disabled, you sign out or registration is found invalid. If permissions or network errors prevent completion, contact the operator to request deletion.
  • Kakao authentication cookies: Sign-in flow cookies last up to ten minutes; result-delivery cookies last up to 60 seconds. They are cleared when authentication finishes.
  • Email inquiries: Deleted after the inquiry or rights request and necessary follow-up checks are completed. If additional retention is needed, its purpose and period will be explained separately.
  • Provider logs and backups: Information separately managed for security and recovery is deleted according to provider policies and service settings. Some Firebase authentication and push information can take up to 180 days after a deletion request to be fully removed from operational systems and backups.

When the purpose is fulfilled or a deletion request is carried out, only necessary information is checked before electronic records are deleted so that ordinary recovery or reuse is not possible. Information that must be kept by law is retained separately, with its legal basis and period explained, and is not used for other purposes.

5. Your rights

You or your lawful representative may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing or withdrawal of consent from the privacy contact. The operator verifies identity or representation and provides results following legally required procedures and deadlines. Reasons will be explained if a request must be restricted or refused.

  • Change character names and goals in Settings, and edit or delete eligible workout records from the record screen.
  • Disable push notifications in Friends and in browser or device notification permissions.
  • Full account deletion is currently requested by email. Email wkdeoghq@gmail.com with “Account deletion request,” your sign-in method, character name, account tag and other information needed to identify your account. Do not send passwords, sign-in tokens or identity document copies in advance.
  • Signing out or disconnecting the app from Google or Kakao does not delete all workout, friend and ranking data. Clearing device storage and deleting your server account are separate actions.

6. Cookies and device storage

The Service uses browser localStorage, IndexedDB and similar storage for workouts, account-specific sync status, sign-in sessions, sound, guide and push preferences. Short-lived cookies protect Kakao sign-in and deliver its results.

You can inspect, block or delete cookies and stored data in your browser's privacy and site-data settings. Blocking may limit sign-in, saving records and retaining settings. Clearing storage can erase unsynced guest records. On a shared device, sign out and clear the site's stored data.

7. Advertising and personalization

The Service uses Google AdSense to connect web advertising. Actual ad display depends on site approval and AdSense settings. Ad requests are made only after checking sign-in status and the account's ad-free entitlement. Development previews do not contact an advertising network.

Google, third-party ad providers and ad networks may use cookies or similar technology to show ads based on visits to this and other websites. IP addresses, page URLs, browser and device information, cookie and advertising identifiers, and ad views or interactions may be sent to those providers.

The operator does not directly pass sign-in account identifiers, email, original workouts or friend lists to AdSense code. Google's policies govern its advertising cookies, personalization and service protection processing.

  • Manage personalized ads in Google My Ad Center.
  • Some third-party personalization can be managed on the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page. Coverage varies by provider and browser.
  • Cookies can also be restricted in browser settings. Disabling personalization does not stop all ads or all processing for security.

Overseas advertising processing uses infrastructure operated by Google and other providers. See Google's partner-site data use information and Google's privacy policy for categories, retention, deletion and settings. Additional provider information and necessary consent and withdrawal tools will be provided when adding providers or serving regions that require consent.

8. Safeguards

Production uses HTTPS. Original account records are protected by authentication and access rules. The server checks permissions for friend relationships and push tokens; public ranking responses include only necessary summaries. Server credentials are not embedded in webpages. Kakao authentication cookies use Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite attributes.

Only the minimum information necessary is used to verify identity for inquiries, and access to material containing personal information is restricted. Refer to provider information for details on their infrastructure protection and encryption.
